# 🏛️ Global Youth Biosecurity Taskforce: Founding Charter

## 🌍 Mission Statement
The Global Youth Biosecurity Taskforce is an independent research and engineering collective dedicated to mitigating digital-to-physical biosecurity vulnerabilities in open-access synthetic biology. 

## ⚙️ Operational Divisions

### 1. Engineering & Technical Infrastructure
* **Focus:** Developing open-source, cloud-native screening firewalls.
* **Current Deployment:** Autonomous API-Level Biosecurity Screening Firewall featuring real-time 6-frame translation and codon-aware threat analysis.

### 2. Governance & Policy Frameworks
* **Focus:** Addressing regulatory blind spots in cloud-based DNA synthesis platforms, including frame-shift exploits and codon degeneracy.
* **Deliverables:** Open-access white papers and institutional compliance standards.

### 3. Open-Access Adoption
* **Focus:** Lowering the barrier to entry for academic labs, startups, and cloud providers to integrate automated biosecurity checks without proprietary costs.
